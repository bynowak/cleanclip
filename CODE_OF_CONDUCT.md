# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss behavior and evidence rather than personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and deliberate publication of private clipboard contents are not acceptable.

Keep examples synthetic and discussion relevant to the project. Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions or restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate and consistent.

Report concerns privately to business@bynowak.com. Include links and relevant context without forwarding unrelated sensitive information. Maintainers will respect the privacy of reports and assess conflicts of interest before acting.
